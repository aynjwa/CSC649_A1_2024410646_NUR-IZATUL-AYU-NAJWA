## concepts 
- Computer vision is an AI technology that enables computers to analyse and understand images.
- Image classification involves categorising images based on their visual characteristics.
- Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) is a deep learning technique that learns visual features from labelled images.
- In this project, computer vision and CNN are used to analyse uploaded chili leaf images and classify them into healthy or selected disease categories.

## Frameworks, models, methods and tools
- CNN: Automatically learns image features to classify chili leaf diseases.
- MobileNetV2: A lightweight CNN architecture considered for classify chili leaf images.
- TensorFlow: Provides tools to implement and train the deep learning model.

## Fit to your problem 
- Computer vision analyses the uploaded chili leaf images to predict the possible diseases and display relevant advisory informtion and preventive measures.
- Benefits: Faster disease identification, convenient access through a mobile application and early advisory support for users.
- Limitations and risks: Limited training images, image quality is important and may predict wrongly.
- Comparison: Computer vision is selected because it can automatically analyse chili leaf images and provide predicted disease categories and helping users who have limited knowledge or experience.
